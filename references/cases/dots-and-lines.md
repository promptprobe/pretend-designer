# Dots & Lines

- Reference: https://www.dotsandlines.io/
- Observed: 2026-09-04 at 1440×1000 and 375×812
- Direction: typographic portfolio + photographic editorial
- Confidence: high for visible layout and computed styles; project order and imagery may change.

## Job and thesis

The site makes a design-and-technology studio memorable before asking visitors to browse work or make contact. Its thesis is a large-format typesetting specimen interrupted by real project photography.

## Measured evidence

- The site exposes custom families named `normal` and `bold` in computed styles.
- Major desktop navigation and display text reached about 166.4px with 174.72px line height; the hero wordmark treatment used about 149.76px line height and roughly -4px tracking.
- Section labels reached about 80px/84.8px; large editorial statements used about 60px/63px.
- The sampled geometry was almost entirely 0px radius; circular or pill controls used an effectively infinite radius.
- The page contained 73 images, six SVGs, and no canvas elements in the observed state.
- The palette is nearly binary black and white, with a saturated orange contact band as the rare accent.

## Page geometry and image treatment

The composition spends large areas on whitespace, then allows words to crop against the viewport or collide with the next section. Photography is wide, editorial, and materially different from project to project. A three-column project row behaves as a gallery strip rather than three generic feature cards: images touch the module edges, labels are subordinate, and the grid is part of a longer visual sequence.

The persistent identity and contact/menu controls remain tiny relative to the page-scale type. The orange contact rail pins a commercial action to the bottom without forcing every section to carry a CTA.

## Mobile behavior

At 375px, the same words become a compact stacked headline rather than a miniature desktop poster. The image crop changes, navigation reduces to a mail icon and menu label, and the next oversized statement enters the first screen. Mobile preserves the tension between type, crop, and whitespace.

## Why it works

The site commits to scale. It does not compensate for large type with excessive decoration. Black, white, crop, and photographic rhythm create recognizability; the orange band appears rarely enough to own the action layer.

## Transferable rules

- Extreme type scale works when the rest of the palette and chrome become quieter.
- Crop words or images at a structural boundary, not randomly.
- Let the project photography vary while maintaining one consistent layout rhythm.
- A single persistent commercial band can replace repeated CTA buttons.
- Whitespace can be an active field when the logo, headline, and image have strong anchors.

## Do not copy

Do not reuse the stacked studio name, exact black/orange identity, German copy, client imagery, or the specific “and solutions” phrase. Oversized sans-serif text alone is not the system; the crop and photographic pacing are equally important.
